Shipping Policy

Last updated: 01 June 2026

At Gurii, we want your order to arrive quickly and safely. Below you will find all the details about our shipping methods, costs, and delivery times across the United Kingdom.

📍 Where We Ship

We currently ship to all addresses within the United Kingdom, including England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. Unfortunately, we do not offer international shipping at this time.

⏱️ Delivery Times (7 days standard)

📦 Standard Delivery: 3–7 business days after dispatch.
⚡ Express Delivery: 1–2 business days (additional fee calculated at checkout).
🆓 Free Standard Delivery: On all orders over £40.

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days (Monday to Friday, excluding UK public holidays). You will receive a dispatch confirmation email with tracking information once your parcel is on its way.

💰 Shipping Costs

  • Standard Delivery (under £40): £3.99
  • Standard Delivery (over £40): FREE
  • Express Delivery: £5.99 (flat rate)

🔍 Order Tracking

Every order includes a tracking number. You can follow your parcel’s journey via our courier partner’s website using the link provided in your shipping confirmation email. If you haven’t received tracking within 3 business days, please contact our support team.

🚚 Delivery Issues

If your order has not arrived within the estimated timeframe (7 business days for standard shipping), please email us at guriltd.official@gmail.com with your order number. We will investigate and keep you updated every step of the way.

In rare cases of lost or stolen parcels, we will file a claim with the courier and offer a replacement or full refund as soon as the claim is resolved.

📮 Incorrect or Undeliverable Address

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us due to an incorrect address provided by the customer, we will contact you to arrange reshipment. Additional shipping charges may apply.

🎁 Multiple Items / Split Shipments

If you order multiple items, they may arrive in separate packages depending on warehouse locations. You will receive separate tracking information for each shipment.

❄️ Extreme Weather & Delays

During periods of severe weather or courier strikes, deliveries may take longer than usual. We appreciate your patience and will communicate any major disruptions on this page.

Still have questions about shipping? Contact our customer service team – we’re happy to help.
